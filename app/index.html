    Roadmap to the Platinum

    One playthrough is enough — there are no online trophies, no mission replay and no gold medals. Difficulty about 4/10, roughly 200 hours. Follow the stages in order; the only thing you must do early is the Chapter 1 meditation.

    Stage 1 — Do first, Chapter 1: Positive Mental Attitude

    Chapter 1, right after the main mission “Down to Earth”.

    After “Down to Earth”, talk to Lucia in the kitchen in the morning. She says she will meditate and walks to the balcony — follow her and choose “Accept”. This is the only possibly missable trophy in the game: it adds +1 to the Progress Menu counter but is not marked as a Meditation Spot like the rest, and it is unconfirmed whether it is required. PowerPyx's author did it and then earned the trophy — so do it. It costs one minute and protects the platinum.

    Stage 2 — The story: chapters 1–5

    60–80 hours of story; free-roam continues after the finale.

    Play the main missions straight through. All 12 story trophies (chapters, Gone Fishing, Breakfast Run, The Cartel Heist, Child's Play, Headliners, VCIA Takedown, Operation Savage Glory, the final mission) unlock automatically and cannot be missed. Sample activities as you go so Master of None ticks over, and bank stunts, races and collectibles you pass — but nothing story-related can lock you out.

    Stage 3 — Stranger strands

    Unlocks with story progress; quest givers return in free-roam.

    Finish the five named stranger finals — Andy (Road to Rehabilitation, ending “Puff Puff Pass”), Kenton (This Was No Boat Accident), Billy (The Lone Rider), Randy (Supply & Demand) and Tyrone (Spin On This!) — plus three more stranger strands to reach 8 for Strangers & Freaks. Hunt Bret's unique animal for Fair Game while you are out. Stranger mission chains have windows tied to story progress, but all trophy-related givers return in free-roam, so none of this is missable.

    Stage 4 — Collectibles

    Best done region by region, alongside exploration.

    • Meditate at every spot (18) — Positive Mental Attitude, started in Stage 1.
    • Complete 3 Collectible Card sets — Don't Touch My Deck.
    • WAiNK It at 100 locations — You WAiNKer!
    • Explore all 52 areas of Leonida — The Goodtime State.
    • Discover 100 vehicles — Whip It Good; register 10 in the Scooter Bros app — On the Spot.
    • Buy all 8 garage properties — Little Boxes.

    Stage 5 — Activities & grind

    Free-roam, any order.

    • 250 unique stunts (Cunning Stunts) · gold all 42 Shooting Range challenges (The Shootist) · win a Top Fun Drone Race at all 14 locations (Top Fun) · win 20 different races (Earn Cash, Not Kudos).
    • Beat Cal at fishing, finish an arcade game, go bowling at Kolt & Associates, max strength with both Jason and Lucia (Personal Best).
    • Rob 5 bank vaults, an armored truck, 3 shops back-to-back with your partner, and one illegal operation per gang (9 gangs) — plus the 4-star wanted escape with your partner, the stealth jewelry robbery, 5 takedowns in one Combat Focus, and the unscheduled VCIA departure.
    • Launder $250,000 in Marked Bills (Dime on the Dollar) and spend $100,000 in shops (Treat Yo Self).
    • Complete 50 of the 80 challenges (One Last Thing) — most finish themselves while you chase the rest.

    Stage 6 — Cleanup & the unconfirmed five

    After the story, when the 100% checklist becomes viewable.

    Open the completion checklist and mop up whatever categories are short — that is A Long Road (100%), the longest grind and the hardest trophy. Then try the five trophies whose methods are still unconfirmed (In The Beginning…, Capital Offense, Bad Luck I Guess, The Truth Is Out There, Ten Pin) and check the guides' updates before trusting any single reported trick. When the last trophy pops, the platinum — Vice Got a Price! — is yours.

    Missables

    Good news first: only one trophy in the whole game can possibly be missed — and even it is unconfirmed. Everything else stays available in free-roam after the story.

    ⚠ The one possibly missable trophy — Positive Mental Attitude (Bronze)

    What: Meditate at every spot across Leonida (18 spots).

    Why it might be missable: In Chapter 1, after the main mission “Down to Earth”, talk to Lucia in the kitchen in the morning. She says she will meditate and goes to the balcony — follow her and choose “Accept”. That session adds +1 to the Progress Menu counter, but it is NOT marked as a “Meditation Spot” like the rest, and it is unconfirmed whether it counts toward the trophy.

    Our call: DO IT in Chapter 1. PowerPyx's author did it and then earned the trophy. It takes one minute; skipping it risks the whole platinum. Then meditate at every marked spot in the world. If you are already past Chapter 1, do not panic — the requirement is unconfirmed, and the other 17+ spots may be enough. Check the guide's updates as players confirm more.

    Sources: PowerPyx — GTA 6 Trophy Guide & Roadmap · KeenGamer — GTA 6 Trophy Guide & Platinum Roadmap

    Missable content that does NOT affect any trophy

    These can disappear permanently, but none of them blocks a trophy or 100% completion:

    • Optional missions that vanish (black icons, pink letters).
    • Some fences becoming permanently unavailable.
    • A few magazines inside story buildings that later close.

    Source: PowerPyx. You can safely ignore all of it on the road to the platinum.

    Things that are NOT missable (despite looking like it)

    • All 12 story trophies — the story is linear and free-roam continues after the finale.
    • All five stranger strands — quest givers return in free-roam even though their chains have story-tied windows.
    • Every collectible and activity — all still accessible after the story; the 100% checklist only becomes viewable after it, but progress made during the story counts.
