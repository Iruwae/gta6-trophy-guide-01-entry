      Roadmap to the platinum

      One playthrough is enough, everything is offline, and free-roam continues after the story. Work the stages in order — the only thing that must happen early is Stage 1. Difficulty 4/10, platinum about 200 hours.

      Missables

      There is exactly one possibly missable trophy in GTA VI. Everything else can be finished in free-roam after the story.

      Missable content that does NOT affect any trophy

      • Optional missions can disappear. Black map icons and pink letters mark optional missions that vanish as the story moves on — none of them is tied to a trophy or to 100% completion.
      • Some fences become permanently unavailable. Losing a fence is an inconvenience for selling, not a trophy blocker.
      • A few magazines sit inside story buildings that close. They are flavour collectibles for the world, not trophy collectibles, and losing access to them blocks nothing.
      • Stranger quest givers have windows tied to story progress — but every trophy-related giver returns in free-roam, so a strand that stalls during the story can still be finished after the finale.

      Cheats disable trophies and saving — keep them off for the whole run. There is no mission replay and no mission gold medals, so nothing about a mission's rating can be missed either.
