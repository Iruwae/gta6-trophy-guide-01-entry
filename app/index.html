Roadmap in 3 steps

  1. Story first (60–80h). Play all 5 chapters. In Chapter 1, after the mission Down to Earth, talk to Lucia in the kitchen in the morning, follow her to the balcony and choose Accept — the only possibly missable trophy interaction.
  2. 100% completion (~100h). After the story, open Progress > Stats > 100% Completion. Earlier progress counts. Targets include 250 stunts (300 for 100%), 42 Shooting Range golds, 39 Gang Operations, 22 Bank Jobs, 8 vaults, 100 WAiNK scans, 100 Cocaine Flamingos, 70 of 80 challenges.
  3. Strangers & cleanup (10–20h). Finish the 8 qualifying Stranger strands (Andy, Kenton, Billy, Randy, Tyrone and others), the 18 meditations, and whatever is left.

Do NOT use cheats — cheats disable trophies and saving (GTABase, TechWiser). There is no mission replay and no mission gold medals, so keep a manual save at the start and end of each chapter. All 55 trophies are offline, single-player; one playthrough is enough.
