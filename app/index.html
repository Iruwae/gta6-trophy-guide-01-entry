Roadmap to the platinum — about 200 hours

One playthrough is enough. All 55 trophies are offline and single-player, there is no mission replay and no gold medals, and free-roam continues after the story with everything for 100% still accessible. The order below is built from the official descriptions: missable risk first, then story, then strangers, then collectibles, then activities, then cleanup. Cheats disable trophies and saving — do not use them.

    Chapter-by-chapter notes

    • Chapter 1 — do the meditation first. After the mission Down to Earth, talk to Lucia in the kitchen in the morning, follow her to the balcony and accept. It is the only step in the whole list that may not come back.
    • Chapters 1–4 — play the story, sample the world. Try a few activities as they appear (Master of None needs nine types), start discovering vehicles, and pick up Stranger strands when their icons appear. Nothing here is missable except the Chapter 1 meditation.
    • Chapter 5 — finish the story. The final mission pops the gold trophy You Gotta Know When to Get Off. The 100% completion checklist becomes viewable after the story.
    • Free-roam cleanup — strangers, then collectibles, then activities. Finish the eight Stranger strands, sweep the 52 areas, 100 WAiNK locations, card sets and 100 vehicles, then grind stunts (250), the Shooting Range golds (42), races and the money trophies. A Long Road (100%) is usually the last trophy before the platinum.

    Missables — only one trophy carries any risk

    Positive Mental Attitude — meditate at every spot (18 spots)

    Do it first, in Chapter 1. After the main mission Down to Earth, talk to Lucia in the kitchen in the morning. She says she will meditate and goes to the balcony — follow her and choose Accept. That moment adds +1 to the Progress Menu counter, but it is not marked as a Meditation Spot like the other 17 spots are.

    Why it matters: it is unconfirmed whether that Chapter 1 meditation is required for the trophy. PowerPyx's author did it and then earned the trophy; no guide has confirmed earning the trophy without it. Our call: do it in Chapter 1, then meditate at every marked spot you find afterwards.

    Last chance: Chapter 1 only — the kitchen moment happens once, in the morning after Down to Earth. Everything else in the game can be done in free-roam after the story.

    Sources: PowerPyx — GTA 6 Trophy Guide & Roadmap · KeenGamer — GTA 6 Trophy Guide & Platinum Roadmap

    Stranger strands — windows, but no lockout

    Stranger mission chains have windows tied to story progress, but every trophy-related quest giver returns in free-roam. If a strand icon disappears mid-story, it comes back after the story — finish it then.

    Missable content that does NOT affect any trophy

    • Optional missions that disappear (black icons, pink letters) — none blocks a trophy or 100%.
    • Some fences becoming permanently unavailable — no trophy needs them.
    • A few magazines in story buildings that close later — not part of any trophy or the 100% checklist.

    Sources: PowerPyx · KeenGamer
